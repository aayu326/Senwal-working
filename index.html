We operate under strict NDA frameworks with every client and team member. Our infrastructure uses encrypted communication channels, access-controlled systems, and secure file-sharing environments. We follow international data governance standards and can accommodate client-specific security requirements, including VPN-only access and dedicated secure workstations.
All substantive legal work at Sanwal & Associates is performed or directly supervised by qualified advocates enrolled with the Bar Council of India. We do not delegate legal analysis to non-lawyers. Our team also includes paralegals, CA professionals, and specialists for specific practice areas — all working under qualified attorney supervision.
We primarily serve clients in the US, UK, EU, UAE, Singapore, and Australia.
